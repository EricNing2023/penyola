What's inside

Made for walking around, not reading at home

  • Hand-drawn maps you can pinch and explore
  • 129 illustrated highlights, each with its own story
  • 150+ audio stories in two English voices
  • Suggested routes with walking times between stops
  • Visitor information: opening hours, ticket notes and entrances
  • A Chinese address card to show your taxi driver
  • Fully offline: no account, no sign-in, no ads
